English Practice

A single-file Vue 3 web app for learning English through contextual translation. Translate Chinese sentences into English, word by word, and instantly see the grammar breakdown, usage context, and similar sentences.

Built with Vue 3 via CDN — no build step, no backend, no dependencies. Just open the page and start practicing. Progress is saved locally in your browser.

Each sentence teaches more than the translation: every word comes with its phonetic transcription, part of speech, Chinese meaning, and grammatical role. After answering correctly, the full analysis reveals how the words work together, when to use the sentence in real life, and two alternative sentences using the same pattern.

The interface is intentionally minimal: a Chinese prompt at the top, a row of word-by-word inputs below. Press Space to commit each word and advance, Enter to submit. Get a word wrong and the bottom border shakes to let you know — without interrupting your flow.

120 questions across everyday topics — daily greetings, work, travel, shopping, social situations, and daily life. No categories, no menus, no progress bars. Just the sentence, the words, and your keyboard.
